Building Permit Packet

Fill this out on your phone, step by step. Every question has a ? button that explains it in plain language. You can only move forward when a step is complete — that keeps your packet from being delayed for missing information.

Before you start — have these handy

  • Owner and contractor names, addresses, and phone numbers
  • The job address, what the work is, what it will cost, and its size in square feet
  • License numbers for every trade doing work (electrical, plumbing, heat/air, roofing)
  • Photos or files of your plans, if you have them — you can also draw your site plan right in the form
  • If you are placing a home on a lot: the year of the home, your deed or signed purchase paperwork, and the home invoice
How long does approval take? Please allow 5 business days for residential permits and 14–28 business days for commercial permits. Call 918-675-4373 for inspections. An incomplete application can delay issuance — this form will not let you submit an incomplete one.
